## Project Overview

This project analyzes a global retail dataset to evaluate sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and product performance. Using SQL for data analysis and Power BI for visualization, the project answers key business questions and provides actionable recommendations to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Business Problem

Management wanted to understand:

* How is overall sales performance changing over time?
* Which regions, countries, and customer segments generate the highest revenue?
* Which product categories contribute the most to profit?
* How do discounts affect profitability?
* Who are the company's most valuable customers?
* Which products require management attention?
* What recommendations can improve overall business performance?

## Dataset

**Dataset:** Global Superstore

## Methodology

The project followed this workflow:

Raw Dataset → Data Cleaning → SQL Analysis → Power BI Dashboard → Business Insights → Business Recommendations

---

## Dashboard Pages

### Executive Dashboard

Provides an overview of business performance through key KPIs, sales trends, regional performance, category performance, and customer segment analysis.

### Profitability Dashboard

Analyzes profit by category and region, identifies loss-making products, and examines the relationship between discounts and profitability.

### Customer Dashboard

Highlights the highest-value customers and compares customer segment performance.

### Time Analysis Dashboard

Explores monthly and quarterly sales trends to identify seasonal patterns and long-term business growth.

---

## Key Insights

* Sales increased consistently between 2011 and 2014.
* The Central region generated the highest revenue.
* The Consumer segment contributed the highest sales.
* Technology was the most profitable product category.
* Higher discounts were associated with lower profitability.
* A small group of customers accounted for a significant share of total revenue.
* Several high-value products consistently generated losses.

---

## Business Recommendations

* Prepare inventory and marketing campaigns ahead of peak sales periods.
* Optimize discount policies to protect profit margins.
* Investigate low-margin markets despite strong sales.
* Strengthen relationships with high-value customers.
* Review pricing and costs for consistently loss-making products.
